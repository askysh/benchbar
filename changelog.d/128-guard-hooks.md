### For contributors

- The Claude Code hooks in `.claude/` are tighter and tested. `guard-rm.sh` now also blocks `rm -r` without `-f`, a delete behind backticks or `$(`, `\rm`, a command substitution as the target of a delete, `git clean`, `git reset --hard`, `find -delete`, `rsync --delete`, text with a delete piped to `sh` or `bash` and an interpreter one liner that deletes, and `~/.benchbar` counts only for its logs. `guard-writes.sh` also blocks `gh` writes to other repositories (also with `-R` or `GH_REPO` before the verb, and `--org` on `gh secret` and `gh variable`), and no longer reads an awk `-F` after a pipe as a body flag. `.claude/settings.json` asks before `git push`, `git tag`, `gh pr merge`, `gh run rerun`, `gh release`, `gh repo delete|archive|rename|edit`, a `gh api` merge or ref delete and `sudo`, denies `bench update`, `bench restore` and `mariadb` or `mysql` as root, and names no Windows user. Under a bypass mode `ask` does nothing and the hooks do not cover push, tag or merge by design. `tests/test-guards.sh` runs a table of allowed and blocked lines against both hooks.
