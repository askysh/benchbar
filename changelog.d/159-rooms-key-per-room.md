### Mac app

- Your Mac shows every room's host a key of its own. Before, every room saw the same key, so two
  hosts could tell that the same Mac joined both. **Forget** deletes a room's key. Rooms you joined
  with 0.8.3 keep working with the key they know: nothing must be done again (#159).
- The join sheet of an invite link shows the address the link leads to and the first 16 digits of
  the host's key, so you can compare the key with the one your host reads out before you join (#159).
