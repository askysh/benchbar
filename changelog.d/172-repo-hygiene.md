### Docs

- Troubleshooting lists four known issues with their workarounds or issue links: two runs at once on a system with uutils `mkdir`, one keepalive at a time on Windows, the unsigned Windows programs, and the unchecked Intel Mac.
- A SUPPORT.md says where to ask a question, report a bug or a docs problem, and that a new issue gets a first reply within two days.

### For contributors

- A "PR text" check fails a pull request whose title has an em or en dash, or whose title, description or commits carry a tool trailer (a Co-Authored-By for a tool or bot, a "Generated with" line). CONTRIBUTING.md has the rule and the failure message points to it.
- A new issue from the bug form gets the label of its Platform: `platform:macos`, `platform:linux` or `windows`.
