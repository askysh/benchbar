### Command line tool

- `benchbar doctor` is much faster: on a bench with 41 apps `doctor --json` went from about 10 s to about 4 s on an M4 (issue #147; `--quick` takes about 2 s). It reads what its checks share once (one `lsof` for every port, one `brew info` for the formulae, one `launchctl print`, one `git config` and one read of each config file per app), starts the slow checks (`bench version`, `brew info`, Python and Node probes, the site ping) side by side, asks git about the apps several at a time, and does its text work in bash instead of starting `awk`, `sed`, `grep` and `tr` for it. The checks, their order and their messages are the same. `FL_DOCTOR_JOBS=1` runs everything one after the other. An interrupted run stops its background jobs and removes its temporary folder.
