### Mac app

- Quick Switch has a key recorder in Settings > Labs: click the shortcut, press a new one (it needs ⌃ or ⌥; Escape cancels). A key another app holds shows a message and keeps the old key, Reset brings back ⌃⌥U, and the choice is remembered. Keys without a letter are named as printed, such as Space or F5 (UI-17, part of #164).
