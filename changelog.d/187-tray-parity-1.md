### Windows tray

- The tray asks the CLI what it can do (`commands --json`) and offers a command only when the CLI inside the distro lists it, so an older CLI never gets a call it would refuse. New in a bench's Overview, under **...**: **Start Services Only** and **Stop Services** (`up --services-only` and `down`, for a restore or a migrate), **Install Service...** or **Refresh Service...**, **Write Lockfile...**, **Apply Lockfile...**, **Watch Assets in Terminal** and **Run in Foreground in Terminal**, and a **Start again after Ubuntu restarts** box (`autostart on` and `off`). The service, the scheduler box and the lockfile calls show what they will do first and run only after your yes; the service and the scheduler use the plan and its token, the lockfile sheet shows the dry run's text, because the CLI has no plan for it yet. The Health tab has **Acknowledge** on a warning and **Undo Acknowledge** on an acknowledged one, from your click only. The New Bench page of the setup wizard lists the warnings and failures of `doctor --prerequisites` for the folder, with the fix to copy (#187).
