### Windows tray

- The More menu of a bench's Overview has the end of a bench's life (#183): **Tidy Up...** marks the bench scratch for 1 to 90 days (`bench scratch`) or not, lists the scratch benches that expired (`bench gc --dry-run`) and the databases no site refers to (`db orphans`), and **Remove Expired...** runs the plan of `bench gc` by its own token. **Forget Bench...** and **Remove Bench...** show the plan of `bench forget` and `bench remove` first; Remove asks you to type the folder name. **Remove Service...** is `uninstall-service` for that one bench, after its dry run. Each entry shows only when the CLI lists its command. The tray never drops a database and never edits the Windows hosts file, so `site hosts --prune` has no entry there.
