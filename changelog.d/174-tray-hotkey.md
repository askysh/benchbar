### Windows tray

- Quick Switch has an opt in global shortcut: **Settings > General > Open Quick Switch with a shortcut**, off until you turn it on, Ctrl+Alt+U by default. Click the shortcut to record another one (it must contain Ctrl or Alt, and Ctrl with a letter needs Alt too); **Reset** goes back to the default. A key another app holds keeps the old one and says so (#174, #167).
