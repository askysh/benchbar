### Mac app

- The ⋯ menu of a bench's Overview has the end of a bench's life (#183): **Tidy Up…** marks the bench scratch for 1 to 90 days (`bench scratch`) or not, lists the scratch benches that expired (`bench gc --dry-run`), the databases no site refers to (`db orphans`) and the hosts lines no bench uses (`site hosts --prune --dry-run`), and **Remove Expired…** runs the plan of `bench gc` by its own token. **Forget Bench…** and **Remove Bench…** show the plan of `bench forget` and `bench remove` first; Remove asks you to type the folder name. **Remove Service…** is `uninstall-service` for that one bench, after its dry run. Each entry shows only when the CLI lists its command. The app never drops an orphan database and never edits `/etc/hosts`: the stale hosts lines come with `benchbar site hosts --prune` to run in Terminal.
- **Run Bench Command…** runs the request its plan was read for, not what the form says by then. The running operations of the Overview match a bench folder reached through a symlink, and keep their list when a read is cancelled.
